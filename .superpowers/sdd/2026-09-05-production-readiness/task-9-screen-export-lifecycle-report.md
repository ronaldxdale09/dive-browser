# DiveScreen frontend export lifetime

Scope: `apps/desktop/src/screen/export.ts` and new `export.lifecycle.test.ts`. No Stage, backend or IPC changes in this slice.

The former exporter could remain indefinitely in seek, encoder back-pressure or encoder flush after cancellation, and failures after media readiness bypassed encoder/media cleanup. Readiness left listeners and timers behind; a delayed play completion could pause the Stage after its exporter released ownership. An encoding exception also leaked the current VideoFrame. The frontend now bounds readiness at 20 seconds, seek/drain/flush/configuration at 10 seconds per wait, and rejects promptly when AbortSignal fires. Every wait removes its listeners/timers in finally, including a late rejection after a timeout. Back-pressure occurs before creating a frame. Every created frame closes even if encode throws; the outer finally closes the encoder and releases owned fallback media on all success/error/cancel paths. A fallback is paused, src removed, load reset, and removed from the DOM. The borrowed Stage video is paused but never reloaded, detached or stripped of src.

Fourteen controlled tests cover ready/seek/drain/flush cancellation and deadline, encoder/frame cleanup after synchronous encode failure, fallback cleanup after canvas failure and success, borrowed preview preservation, late play resolution, and cancellation during the final upload append preventing native finish from starting. Initial clean RED: 9 failed / 2 passed with no unhandled errors; final expanded GREEN: 14 exporter tests, plus 15 existing Stage/store/DiveScreen lifecycle tests (29 total), all passed. Scoped ESLint and frontend TypeScript check passed. Logs: `/tmp/dive-screen-export-lifecycle-red.log`, `/tmp/dive-screen-export-lifecycle-green.log`; copies in `target/screen-export-lifecycle-regression/`.

Limits: this is frontend media/encoder ownership, not native-job cancellation. In-flight IPC writes and ffmpeg are deliberately not raced against AbortSignal: rejecting a frontend promise cannot prove native work stopped. Abort is rechecked before additional upload work and before native finish, including after the final append. A finish already running must still finish or return its native error. Native job cancellation/deadline, abandoned staged-file removal, dialog lifetime across in-flight native finish, and atomic output publication remain separate backend/IPC work. No native build/run was performed here, and run14's decode error cause remains unproved. Parent owns native qualification. Independent source review found no actionable issue; it confirmed wait cleanup, late-callback protection, per-frame/encoder/fallback ownership, borrowed-source preservation and the last-append cancellation check. Native decoder/ffmpeg cancellation is not proved by these tests.
